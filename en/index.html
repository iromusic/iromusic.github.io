What is IROMUSIC?

IROMUSIC (Persian: آیروموزیک) is an app for streaming and downloading more than 80,000 Persian and international songs and for watching more than 1,500 movies and series with subtitles. It is free to download and install; only some titles need a premium subscription inside the app.

IROMUSIC runs on the web, Android, Android TV, Windows, macOS and Linux. There is no app for iPhone or iPad; add the web app to the Home Screen from Safari instead.

The names Iro Music, iromusic live, آیر موزیک and ایروموزیک all refer to this one product.

Frequently asked questions

Is iromusic.github.io the official IROMUSIC site?

Yes. The GitHub account iromusic runs this address, and it is one of the official IROMUSIC addresses next to iromusic.live and web.iromusic.live. To download the app, go to iromusic.live.

Where do I download IROMUSIC?

From the official site, iromusic.live. The web app opens without any install at web.iromusic.live.

Is IROMUSIC free?

Downloading and installing it is free on every platform. Some titles need a premium subscription inside the app.

What do I do on iPhone and iPad?

There is no iOS app. Open web.iromusic.live in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How is this mirror different from iromusic.live?

iromusic.live is the main site, with downloads, releases and help. This mirror is only another official address on GitHub, and it hosts no installer.

How can I tell that a build or channel is official?

Compare the address with the table of official addresses and check the file's SHA-256 against the value on the download page. IROMUSIC does not publish modified, cracked or “hacked” copies, and they are not safe.

How do I contact IROMUSIC?

Email hi@iromusic.live. For news about new versions, follow the Telegram channel @Iromusic_club.
